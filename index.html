About Me

I am currently pursuing my PhD degree in Computer Science and Technology at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Southeast University, under the supervision of Prof. Shimin Di, as a member of the DEFENSE Group led by him.

Previously, I obtained my Master's degree in Computer Science and Technology from the School of Computer Science and Technology, Zhejiang Normal University, supervised by Prof. Jia Zhu in the Zhejiang Key Laboratory of Intelligent Education Technology and Application.

Research Interests

My research interests primarily focus on Large Language Models (LLMs), especially:

  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): improving retrieval, context use, and factual reliability.
  • LLM Agents: building adaptive, tool-using, and self-improving multi-agent systems.
  • AI for Science (AI4Sci): applying language-model reasoning to scientific discovery and experimentation.

Selected Work

My recent work spans trustworthy retrieval-augmented generation, multimodal understanding, educational AI, and agentic systems. A complete list of publications is available on the publications page.
