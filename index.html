About Me

I am a first-year PhD student in Computer Science and Technology at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Southeast University, under the supervision of Prof. Shimin Di at the DEFENSE Group, PALM Lab. Previously, I obtained my Master's degree in Computer Science and Technology from the School of Computer Science and Technology, Zhejiang Normal University, supervised by Prof. Jia Zhu in the Zhejiang Key Laboratory of Intelligent Education Technology and Application.

I am actively seeking research collaborations and research internship opportunities. If you would like to connect about Large Language Models and Agents, please feel free to contact me by email.
