About me

I am currently pursuing my PhD degree in Computer Science and Technology at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Southeast University, under the supervision of Prof. Shimin Di, as a member of the DEFENSE Group led by him. Previously, I obtained my Master's degree in Computer Science and Technology from the School of Computer Science and Technology, Zhejiang Normal University, supervised by Prof. Jia Zhu in the Zhejiang Key Laboratory of Intelligent Education Technology and Application.

Google Scholar metrics: Citations: 86; h-index: 6; i10-index: 3 (profile).

My research interests focus on Large Language Models (LLMs), with three main directions:

  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): dynamic retrieval, including when to retrieve and what to retrieve.
  • LLM Agents: harness self-evolution, agent tool use, memory, and long-horizon agents.
  • AI for Science (AI4Sci): scientific agents and scientific discovery.
