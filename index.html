About me

I'm an Assistant Professor at the Information Systems Technology and Design (ISTD) Pillar, Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD), under the SAP research award. I'm also a Singapore National Research Foundation (NRF) Fellow (Class of 2026). Before this, I was a research scientist at Alibaba Group Singapore with the Ali Star award. I received my Ph.D. from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Wai Lam.

My research interests lie broadly in natural language processing, large language models, and LLM agents. I lead the iNLP Lab where we aim to build AI systems that work reliably and efficiently for diverse people, languages, and real-world settings. Currently, we focus on:

  • [Multilingual & Multicultural LLMs] Building (and understanding) LLMs for diverse languages and cultural contexts (SeaLLMs, Babel, SeaLLMs-Audio, Abstract-Thought, a recent overview talk)
  • [Social World Models] How AI agents can model social environments, simulate interactions, and predict behavior in culturally and socially diverse settings (MoltNet, MASim)
  • [Efficiency & Small LMs] Making LLMs/agentic systems more efficient and trustworthy under real-world resource constraints (e.g., Custom Pruning, PEAR)
  • [Evaluate -> Improve] Dynamic evaluation in interactive settings (M3Exam, Auto-Arena, DR-Arena)
